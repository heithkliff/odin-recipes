Creamy Buldak Noddles

This is less of a recipe, more a serving suggestion for buldak noodles! I used the extra hot chicken buldak noodles here (the ones in the black packet). And just to flag, these are very hot… the cream and mozzarella manage to mute the heat slightly, but if you want to lean on the side of caution, only add a bit of the sauce packet (then add more if you can handle it)!

  • garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1 pack extra hot chicken buldak noodles (the ones in the black packaging)
  • 375ml boiling water
  • 5 tbsp grated mozarella
  • 2 tbsp double cream
  • 1 spring onion, finely sliced
  • Oil
  • Toppings: boiled egg, chilli flakes, sesame seeds
  1. a medium sized frying pan over a medium heat, add 1 tbsp of oil then the garlic. Fry until golden, stir often.
  2. Remove the noodles from the packet and place in the frying pan. Add the boiling water and turn the heat to high. Simmer until the noodles are cooked and water has mostly evaporated.
  3. Stir in the sauce packet, grated mozzarella and double cream. TIP: these noodles are spicy so only add a bit of the sauce packet if you don't want it too hot!
  4. Stir in the spring onion.
  5. Add your toppings and that’s it!
